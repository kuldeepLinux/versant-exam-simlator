📋 Exam Rules & Guidelines

Please read carefully before starting the exam

⚠️ FOLLOW THESE RULES STRICTLY

  • Use your headphone — Make sure your headphones are properly connected and working.
  • Use a PC / Laptop — Mobile phones are not allowed during the exam.
  • Sit in an empty room — No other person should be present in the room.
  • No noise / silence — The room must be completely quiet with no background noise.
  • Blank wall behind you — Sit in front of a plain, blank wall (no posters, no windows, no people).
  • Do not move your lips — Unless the question asks you to speak, keep your lips still.
  • Keep your eyes on the computer — Do not look away from the screen at any time.
  • No cheating — Do not use any other device, notes, or take help from anyone.
  • Do not leave the screen — Stay on the exam tab until the exam is complete.
  • Stable internet — Make sure your internet connection is stable before starting.
